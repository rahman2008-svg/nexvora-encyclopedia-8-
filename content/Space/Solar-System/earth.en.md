# Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. About 29.2% of Earth's surface is land, and the remaining 70.8% is covered with water.

## Geological Characteristics
- **Atmosphere**: Consisting of 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, and trace gases essential for life.
- **Hydrosphere**: Approximately 71% of Earth's surface is covered by oceans, seas, and freshwater bodies.
- **Magnetic Field**: Generated by the fluid outer core, shielding life from harmful solar radiation.

## Rotation and Orbit
Earth rotates around its axis roughly every 24 hours and orbits the Sun in approximately 365.25 days, causing the cycle of day/night and seasonal shifts.
